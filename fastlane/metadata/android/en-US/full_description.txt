<b>Daymark</b> is an intuitive, privacy-friendly event tracker designed to help you celebrate upcoming milestones and cherish meaningful memories from the past with no accounts, ads, or tracking.

<b>Features:</b>
* <b>Flexible Countdown & Count-Up:</b> Count down to future moments or count up from past milestones with uncapped date range and exact time precision.
* <b>Material You Design:</b> Modern fluid animations, rounded cards, full dark and light mode, and customizable per-event accent colors and icons.
* <b>Recurrence & Smart Anniversaries:</b> Hourly, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly repeats with smart rollover.
* <b>Local Reminders & Notifications:</b> Scheduled directly on your device with customizable alert timings.
* <b>Organization:</b> Category filtering, search, pin to top, custom categories, and gesture navigation.
* <b>Home-Screen Widgets:</b> Interactive widgets with auto-refresh and customizable appearance.
* <b>Complete Privacy:</b> Fully offline, no internet required, zero tracking or analytics, with full JSON backup & restore.
